Marketing and lifecycle experience

How I plan, build and run lifecycle marketing, in four short case studies.

Post-purchase flow redesign

Challenge
The post-purchase flow had significant room to generate more revenue.
Approach
I was tasked with completely redoing every email in the flow, using behavioral segmentation and personalized messaging.
Result
Revenue from the flow increased significantly.

Managing multiple brands

Challenge
Three ecommerce brands needed lifecycle email and SMS running at the same time.
Approach
I owned campaigns and projects end to end for each brand, keeping brand standards consistent across all three.
Result
Owned channels became a meaningful share of monthly revenue.

Segmentation

Challenge
There was no segmentation, so everyone received the same emails.
Approach
I created audience lists based on customer behavior, engagement and purchase history, so people received emails that mattered to them.
Result
Open rates improved significantly, supported by deliverability improvements and more relevant messaging.

Pages, content and ads

Challenge
Funnels and product pages needed to convert visitors and support the campaigns.
Approach
I built and optimized landing pages and product pages, wrote and optimized blog content, and created ads, working closely with customer success.
Result
The pages, content and ads were sent regularly to email segments to generate revenue.
